Trinity Solutions

A done-for-you creative and AI consultancy — built from scratch, brand and all.

Overview

A live proof of concept.

The Problem

Service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical — are losing 20–30% of their leads every day to missed calls and slow follow-up. They know it. They just don't have the time or technical knowledge to fix it. Most agencies either don't understand the trades, or charge enterprise prices for cookie-cutter solutions.

Trinity Solutions is my creative consultancy serving small businesses in the trades and service industries. I'm responsible for everything — the brand identity, the positioning strategy, the website, and the client experience systems.


It's a live proof of concept: I do for my own business exactly what I do for clients. The brand is the portfolio.


Key Decisions

  • Chose a dark, premium SaaS aesthetic over the warm, approachable look most service businesses use — because Trinity's positioning is done-for-you, not do-it-yourself. The design had to communicate that before anyone read a word.
  • Built the entire business on the same AI and automation tools sold to clients — so the capabilities aren't theoretical. Every system on the site is live and running. The brand is the proof of concept.
  • Wrote the copy to speak directly to trade business owners who are tired of agencies — not to people exploring their options. The voice is direct, specific, and assumes the reader has been burned before.

AI Tools & Automation

Trinity isn't just a brand — it's a functioning business built on the same AI and automation tools I sell to clients. Every system below is live, running, and part of how I deliver results at a fraction of the overhead of a traditional agency.

AI Reception

A front desk that never clocks out.

An AI receptionist handles inbound calls, qualifies leads, answers FAQs, and books appointments — 24/7, without a single missed opportunity. For trades businesses, this alone pays for the entire engagement.

CRM & Pipeline

Every lead tracked. Every follow-up sent.

A fully built-out CRM pipeline — from first contact to closed job — with automated follow-ups, job status tracking, and review request sequences. Built in Go High Level and customized for each client's workflow.

Missed Call TextBack

Turn a missed call into a booked job.

When a call goes unanswered, an automated text fires within seconds — keeping the lead warm and giving them an immediate path to book. Most clients recover 20–30% of previously lost leads within the first month.

Every workflow, connected end to end.

From lead capture to closed job, every automation runs in sync — no manual handoffs, no dropped balls. This is the full picture of what's running under the hood at Trinity, and what gets built for every client from day one.
