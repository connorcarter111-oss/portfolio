About

I've never just been a designer.

I'm Connor Carter — a creative director, brand builder, and founder based in Dallas, Georgia. For the past eight years I've been obsessed with the same question: what makes people feel something about a brand?

I've answered it by building specialty coffee companies in Istanbul, designing physical spaces that people make part of their daily ritual, directing photo and video shoots, building websites, and growing organic communities from zero.

I think the best creative work happens when strategy and execution aren't separated — when the person who defined the brand vision is the same one deciding what the chair in the corner should look like. That's how I work.
