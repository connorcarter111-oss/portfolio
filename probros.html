SERVICES

Your Room, Our Expertise.

No two rooms are the same — because no two ministries are. We listen to how you actually use the space, spec a system that fits the room and the volunteers running it, and install it clean. Whether it's a single upgrade or a ground-up build, every detail is engineered around your Sunday.

  • Design-Build: End-to-end design and installation of permanent audio, video, and lighting — a single sanctuary upgrade or a ground-up install.
  • Engineering: We spec the gear, engineer the signal flow, and run the wiring so the whole system works as one.
  • Handoff & Training: Documented, labeled, and turned over to your team with training so volunteers can run it on a Sunday.
  • System Design: Line arrays, point-source, and distributed systems built for the room, not the spec sheet.
  • Intelligibility: Speech-first, feedback-resistant coverage so the back row hears as clearly as the front.
  • Tuning: Room tuning and gain structure that keeps the platform mix clean week after week.
  • Displays: Screens, projectors, and LED walls sized and placed for the space.
  • Cameras & Switching: IMAG, multi-cam, and switching for a service that looks intentional.
  • Media Playback: Lyrics, media, and presentation workflows a small team can drive.
  • Stage & Architectural: Lighting that supports the service instead of distracting from it.
  • Scenes & Presets: Programmed looks your team can trigger without a lighting operator.
  • Control: Simple, reliable control that holds up service after service.
  • Permanent Streaming: Cameras, encoding, and audio routing dialed in so you go live without a scramble.
  • Broadcast Audio: A dedicated stream mix so online church sounds like you meant it.
  • Reliability: Repeatable, documented setups your volunteers can run every week.
